How it works

From signal to decision

  1. A partner publishes

    Your selected source sends an opening or closing signal to a topic you follow.

  2. Your phone checks

    Vyapar Sanket receives the signal and checks your configuration and broker connection.

  3. Your rules apply

    Review eligible openings in Manual or let Automatic handle them. Closing a position always needs your confirmation.

  4. Your broker processes

    Check the order status and resulting position in your own broker account. An order can be delayed, rejected or partially filled.
